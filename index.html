about

I'm a Cambridge-trained innovator from Sri Lanka working at the intersection of technology, policy, and social impact. I founded Coexist — an AI-powered system using LiDAR sensors to detect elephants on railways and prevent fatal collisions. It won the Lucy Cavendish Enterprise Award and earned me selection as a UN Millennium Fellow (top 5% of 52,000 applicants from 170 countries). I serve as VP of Cambridge University Entrepreneurs and as a Global Shaper with the World Economic Forum. I also debate.
